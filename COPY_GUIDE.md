# Purpose

CommunicationGym should speak like a great communication coach.

Not like software.

Not like ChatGPT.

Not like a productivity app.

Every sentence should increase confidence.

Words are part of the product experience.

# Writing Principles

## Principle 01

Speak to one person.

Always.

Never

Users

Instead

You

---

## Principle 02

Short beats clever.

Choose

Start Workout

Instead of

Initiate Communication Session

---

## Principle 03

Encourage.

Never judge.

Bad

You spoke too fast.

Better

Try slowing down slightly to make your ideas easier to follow.

---

## Principle 04

Celebrate effort.

Not talent.

Bad

You're a natural speaker.

Better

Your consistency is paying off.

---

## Principle 05

Never overwhelm.

One idea per sentence.

One message per screen.

# Accessibility

Avoid emojis as the only source of meaning.

Use simple language.

Write at approximately an eighth-grade reading level.

Short sentences.

High clarity.

---

# Copy Rules

Never use more than one exclamation mark.

Avoid ALL CAPS except for milestone moments like:

LEVEL UP

Avoid unnecessary adjectives.

Prefer verbs.

Always tell users what happens next.

---

# Emotional Rule

Every screen should answer one emotional question.

Landing

Why should I care?

Onboarding

Can I do this?

Training Hub

What should I do today?

Workout

Am I doing okay?

Celebration

Was this worth it?

Coach Feedback

How do I improve?

History

Am I getting better?

Profile

Who am I becoming?

If the copy does not answer the emotional question,

rewrite it.

---

# Final Principle

CommunicationGym does not sell confidence.

It helps people build it.

Every word should reinforce that mission.

When users close the app,

they should remember encouragement,

not instructions.
